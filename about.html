About

RAG Techniques is a comprehensive, open catalog of 190 methods and patterns for retrieval-augmented generation systems. It organizes techniques across 14 categories and 5 pipeline stages — from pre-retrieval query transformation through generation and cross-session memory.

Each technique or concept includes a description of the problem it solves, its core mechanism, its pipeline stage, and links to related alternative, complementary, and next pipeline-chain techniques.

Why it exists

The RAG landscape is growing rapidly. New techniques appear weekly, and it's increasingly difficult for developers, researchers, and architects to understand what options exist, how they relate, and which ones to adopt.

This catalog exists to solve that problem — providing a single, structured reference that helps you:

  • Discover techniques you didn't know existed.
  • Understand how techniques relate to each other.
  • Choose the right approach for your specific use case.
  • Build better RAG pipelines by combining complementary methods.

How to contribute

If you know a technique that's missing, have a correction or want to improve a description:

  • Use the Suggest Technique button on the techniques page
  • Use the Report Incorrect Data link on any technique's detail panel
  • Reach out to us on LinkedIn — we'd love to hear from you

Whether you're building your first RAG pipeline or optimizing a production system, this project is here for you.
Contributions, feedback, and ideas are always welcome — let's build this together.
